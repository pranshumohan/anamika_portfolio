Wellness Retreat · High-Ticket · International · Project-Based

48% CPL reduction through a mid-campaign demographic pivot across 8 markets.

The Challenge

A 7-day yoga retreat needed bookings from wellness audiences across India, US, France, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Germany, Israel. Strict CPL ceiling. Previous campaigns had no demographic-level decision-making — a significant efficiency leak.

The Approach

Geo-segmented campaigns per market. Live age/gender data revealed a 2.5× CPL gap: male 25–34 at ₹44.62 vs female 25–34 at ₹111.62. Reallocated budget mid-flight to the high-performing cohort. Blended CPL fell from ₹87.52 to ₹45.61 — a 48% drop without pausing a single ad.

Education · Lead Generation · Oct – Nov 2025

3.4× lead volume at a lower CPL — the A/B campaign architecture in action.

The Challenge

An IIT JEE coaching institute needed high-intent leads within a strict ₹40 CPL ceiling. Previous campaigns: high CPC, low conversion. A creative-to-audience mismatch that needed more than new creative — it needed a different architecture.

The Approach

Deployed Campaign 1 as a deliberate learning vehicle with 7-day click attribution to isolate high-intent signals. Applied every finding to a scaled variant. Result: Campaign 2 delivered 3.4× the leads at a lower CPL than an already on-target Campaign 1. Learning compounds.
